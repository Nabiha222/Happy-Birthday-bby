A letter I never got to say

One Last Thing… 🤍

I don't know if you'll ever read these words, but there is something I want you to know.

I understood you.

I understood what you were trying to tell me. I understood why you had to leave, and I understood that you weren't leaving because you stopped caring. You simply didn't want me to keep waiting for something you couldn't promise.

You asked me to focus on my own life, and I'm trying to keep your words. I'm studying. I'm working on myself. I'm slowly learning how to spend my time without waiting for you.

But none of that changes what you meant to me.

I loved you. I still do. And perhaps a part of me always will.

Not because I'm waiting for you, and not because I expect you to come back. But because some people become a beautiful part of your life simply by existing in it.

You were that person for me.

Thank you for every hour in VC, every little conversation, every moment you stayed, every time you listened, every little thing you remembered, and every moment you made me feel valued.

You taught me what it feels like to be cared for, understood and respected.

And you taught me something even more important: loving someone doesn't always mean holding onto them. Sometimes, loving someone means understanding them enough to let them go.

So I'm going to try. I'm going to keep your words. I'm going to focus on my studies, my dreams, my life, and the person I'm becoming.

I may not know what the future holds, and I won't pretend that I do. But I know one thing for certain: I'm grateful our paths crossed.

Maybe we weren't meant to have a forever together. Maybe we were only meant to have a beautiful chapter.

And honestly… I'm grateful for that chapter.

I hope you take care of yourself, wherever life takes you. I hope your work becomes lighter, your dreams become real, and you find peace in the life you're building.

I won't wait for you anymore.
But I'll always wish you well. 🤍

— From someone who will always be grateful
